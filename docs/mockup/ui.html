tix: mockup in the ASCII style

A sample of meaning, not the exact text. Deviations from the spec: interface and file sections in English (D46); identity in Actor: and claim is the full session id, 8 characters on screen (D56); Kaiten, GitHub and external_state come after the MVP (docs/specs/integrations.md).

Five terminal screens: board, card, what the agent sees, task file, integrations. The board is live: click the terminal and press h j k l, enter, m and M, p, s, esc. A yellow star means your action is needed. Moving a card writes a commit at once, and for a linked task sends the status to Kaiten or GitHub in the background; the bottom line shows both steps. The button "agent at work" shows step by step how agents change the board while you watch. The data is made up.
